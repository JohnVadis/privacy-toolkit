What it can and can't work out

If the PDF has real form fields (like the demo form), every field is found and matched to a client detail by name. This is the reliable case.

If it's a flat scan — which most Florida county forms are — it reads the text layer and places each value against the label it belongs to. On your existing forms that finds most of the values but none of the checkboxes: on these PDFs the boxes aren't text, so there's nothing to find. You place those yourself.

If it's an image with no text at all, nothing can be detected and every value has to be placed by hand.

Either way you get an editor with the form on screen, and nothing is used for a real filing until you've looked at it.
